William Engeler

I have been working as an orientation and mobility specialist for more than 15 years, partnering with individuals, school districts, universities, private companies, and state and federal agencies. My goal is to help you reach your own goal of independence — whatever that looks like for you.

I work at your pace, with encouragement when you're ready to push further.

I serve clients throughout the Maryland and Virginia region.

How I Can Help

  • White Cane Training

    Learn to use a white cane for safe, confident travel — whether you're new to vision loss or looking to refine your technique.

  • Dog Guide Services

    Assistance with dog guide school applications, assessments, and patterning your dog guide in your home environment.

  • Assistive Technology

    Get the most out of your smartphone and the travel apps available today — iPhone or Android, beginner to advanced.

  • Maryland & Virginia Orientation

    New to the area? I can orient you to your neighborhood and to the Metrobus and Metrorail systems.
